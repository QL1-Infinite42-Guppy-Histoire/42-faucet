Rules & notes

  • One claim per address every 7 days, and one claim per network (IP address) every 24 hours — counted separately on each chain.
  • Only addresses holding less than one claim amount can claim. This faucet is for gas relief, not a giveaway for funded wallets.
  • At most 100 claims a day per chain. Distribution pauses automatically when the faucet balance runs low.
  • Never enter a private key or recovery phrase anywhere. This site only needs your public address — anyone asking for a key or phrase is a scam.
  • Amounts are small on purpose: just enough to make a few transactions. Please don't drain it with many wallets.
